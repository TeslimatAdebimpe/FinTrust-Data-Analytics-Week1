# Success Criteria

The Data Analytics solution will be considered successful if:

- The customer and transaction datasets are properly profiled.
- The relationship between the datasets is understood.
- Obvious data-quality issues are documented.
- The analytical questions can realistically be answered using the supplied data.
- KPIs are clearly defined and linked to available data fields.
- The dashboard structure connects to the identified business questions.
- Analytical findings are supported by the available data.
- The final dashboard communicates information clearly to management.
