# Business Understanding

## FinTrust Business Problem

FinTrust Digital Bank is seeking to use data, machine learning and generative AI to improve its understanding of customer and transaction behaviour, support risk-related decision-making, improve operational processes and strengthen digital customer support.

## Data Analytics Problem

The Data Analytics track focuses on determining how FinTrust's customer and transaction data can be transformed into useful business intelligence.

## Why This Matters

Understanding customer and transaction behaviour can support evidence-based business decisions. Analysis of transaction activity, transaction value, channels, status and risk-related patterns can help management understand business performance and identify areas requiring further investigation.

## Role of Data Analytics

The Data Analytics track will:

- Profile the available datasets
- Identify data-quality issues
- Understand relationships between datasets
- Develop meaningful analytical questions
- Define relevant KPIs
- Identify business insights
- Plan a Power BI dashboard
- Provide an analytical foundation for later project stages

## Expected Output

The expected Week 1 output is a Business & Data Intelligence Assessment containing:

- Business understanding
- Data understanding
- Analytical questions
- KPI definitions
- Dashboard wireframe
- Initial analysis plan
