# FinTrust Digital Bank — Data Analytics Week 1

## Project Overview

This repository contains my Week 1 submission for the AnalystLab Africa Experience Lab Internship Programme.

The project focuses on the FinTrust Digital Bank Data Analytics track.

The objective is to understand how FinTrust's customer and transaction data can be transformed into useful business intelligence.

---

## Business Problem

FinTrust Digital Bank wants to use data, machine learning and generative AI to improve its understanding of customer and transaction behaviour, support risk-related decision-making, improve operational processes and strengthen digital customer support.

The Data Analytics track focuses on the business intelligence component of this problem.

---

## Week 1 Objectives

- Understand the FinTrust business problem
- Review available data resources
- Profile the customer and transaction datasets
- Define analytical questions
- Define KPIs
- Design a dashboard wireframe
- Establish an initial Week 2–4 analysis plan
