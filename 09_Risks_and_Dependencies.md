# Risks and Future Dependencies

## Risks

### 1. Missing Values
Missing data may affect KPI calculations and analysis.

### 2. Data Inconsistency
Inconsistent values may produce inaccurate groupings or calculations.

### 3. Incorrect Field Interpretation
Misunderstanding a field may lead to incorrect business conclusions.

### 4. Dataset Relationship Issues
An unsuitable relationship between datasets could affect customer-level transaction analysis.

### 5. Synthetic Data Limitation
The FinTrust data is synthetic and intended for educational purposes, so findings should not automatically be treated as real-world banking behaviour.

### 6. Risk Field Interpretation
The risk-review field should be treated as a synthetic educational label and not as a confirmed fraud determination.

## Future Dependency

The Data Science track may later require transaction insights and analytical findings produced by the Data Analytics track.

Formal cross-track collaboration begins in Week 2.
